## About Anyx

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Anyx?">
    Anyx is an AI agent that works inside WhatsApp. Add it to your WhatsApp groups and it emails you a short briefing of each group: decisions, unanswered questions, action items and what needs you. In a private chat, it is your personal assistant for reminders, routines and your connected apps. See [What is Anyx?](/)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Anyx made by WhatsApp?">
    No. Anyx, Inc. is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta. Anyx is a separate WhatsApp contact that you chat with and add to groups.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it work on iPhone, Android and WhatsApp Web?">
    Yes. Anyx is a regular WhatsApp contact, so it works in any WhatsApp app: iPhone, Android, WhatsApp Web and WhatsApp Desktop. There is nothing to install.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it work in Hebrew?">
    Yes. Anyx understands Hebrew, English and mixed chats. Notes are written in the language of the chat by default, or in one of 20 languages you choose.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can Anyx read all my WhatsApp chats?">
    No. Anyx is not connected to your WhatsApp account. It only sees your private chat with it and the groups you add it to. See [Privacy and security](/security).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do group members see my notes?">
    No. Notes go to your email and your Anyx account, and to any email recipients you add. They are never posted in the group.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Anyx use my data to train AI models?">
    Anyx does not train AI models on your data. To answer you and write notes, Anyx sends message content to third-party AI providers. For the legal details, see the [Privacy Policy](https://anyx.app/privacy).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should I tell the group that I added Anyx?">
    Yes. Let the members know Anyx is there. There is a ready-made message in [Connect a WhatsApp group](/ai-notes/connect-a-group).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## AI Notes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will Anyx talk in my group?">
    Only when someone tags it, for example `@Anyx`. Otherwise it stays quiet. On Pro and Max you can also set up a routine that runs for a group.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How often do notes arrive?">
    Every 24 hours by default. You can choose every 6, 12 or 48 hours, or once a week. The first note is processed on an upcoming scheduled run after you connect the group. If there were no messages during an interval, no email is sent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get notes on WhatsApp instead of email?">
    By default, automatic notes go by email. Every note in your account has a **Send to my WhatsApp** button that sends it to your private chat with Anyx.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Anyx summarize messages from before it joined?">
    Yes, if you import a WhatsApp chat export. Then ask for a note for that date range. See [Import older messages](/ai-notes/settings#import-older-messages).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it understand voice notes?">
    Yes. Voice notes are transcribed, so their content can appear in notes and in answers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to turn notes on?">
    No. For a newly connected group on an active plan with an email on your account, notes and automatic email are already on. Notes go to your account address, every 24 hours by default. You can change the schedule and recipients in the group's **Notes** tab. See [Schedule, language and recipients](/ai-notes/settings).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I tell Anyx what matters in a group?">
    Yes. In the group's **Notes** tab, **What matters in this group?** lets you set the group's context, its importance to you and your own priorities. It applies to future notes. See [Schedule, language and recipients](/ai-notes/settings#tell-anyx-what-matters-in-this-group).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Using Anyx with other AI assistants

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use my notes with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another assistant?">
    Yes, through the email Anyx already sends. Connect the mailbox that receives your notes to the assistant, or paste a note into it. There are guides for ChatGPT and Codex, Claude and Claude Code, Gemini, Grok and Grok Bot, Instinct, Meta Muse, OpenClaw, NanoClaw and Hermes Agent. Not every assistant can read a mailbox or run on a schedule, and features, plans, regions and permissions vary. The guides follow each vendor's own documentation and are not end-to-end tests of your account. See [Use your Notes with an AI assistant](/ai-notes/assistants/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does my AI assistant get access to my WhatsApp groups?">
    It depends on how you connect. The email workflow shares delivered Notes, not direct access to group messages. With a separate Anyx MCP connection, a compatible assistant can read messages Anyx captured from eligible active groups owned by your account. It is not a live WhatsApp feed or a complete archive. See [Connect an AI app with MCP](/developers/mcp-quickstart).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is MCP, and how do I connect it?">
    MCP (Model Context Protocol) is a way for a compatible AI app to read context from Anyx. Open **Settings > Connect your AI (MCP)** in Anyx and follow the setup for your client. Use Anyx sign-in where that client supports it, or a personal key stored in the client's secure configuration. Never paste the key into an AI conversation. Access depends on your account and the client's authentication and tool support, not just whether the app says it supports MCP. See the [MCP quickstart](/developers/mcp-quickstart).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can my AI assistant read files shared in my groups?">
    Through MCP, a compatible client can read eligible original files retained by Anyx, together with their captured source messages. The same authorized key or connected app is used for messages and eligible files; a second media toggle is not required. Saved AI extraction is different from the original file. The client must expose the file tools and be able to read the returned format. Files that were not retained, fall outside your account's history window or are no longer authorized can be unavailable. This is not access to your entire WhatsApp media library. See [Read an available attachment](/developers/mcp-quickstart#read-an-available-attachment).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I limit an MCP key to just one group?">
    No. The connection covers all eligible active groups owned by your account, including groups that become eligible later, and eligible original files within the permitted history window. Naming one group in a prompt only tells the assistant what to use; it does not restrict the key or connected app. Connect only an app you trust with that scope. See [Scope and limits](/developers/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can MCP send messages or read my private chats and Notes?">
    No. This MCP connection is read-only and does not send WhatsApp messages or write to your CRM. Private chats, emailed Notes and imported history are outside its scope. Use the email workflow above for Notes. Your assistant may have other tools with their own permissions, which you should review separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I stop an assistant's MCP access?">
    Open **Settings > Connect your AI (MCP)** to revoke a personal key, disconnect the relevant app or turn off MCP access. Disconnecting an app and revoking a personal key are separate controls. Revocation stops future authorized reads; it cannot erase information the assistant already received. See [MCP troubleshooting](/developers/mcp-troubleshooting).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who sees my notes if I connect an assistant?">
    Email recipients can see the whole note, and an assistant that reads that mailbox can too. Share only notes you are allowed to share with that assistant. See [Privacy and security](/security).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Plans and billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much does Anyx cost?">
    Starter is $35 a month (up to 4 groups), Pro is $60 (up to 30 groups) and Max is $120 (up to 120 groups). Yearly billing is 20% cheaper. See [Plans and free trial](/plans) and the [pricing page](https://anyx.app/pricing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Yes. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial on any plan. If you cancel before it ends, you pay nothing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free plan?">
    Groups, AI Notes and connected apps need Starter, Pro or Max. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I cancel?">
    On [anyx.app](https://anyx.app), open **Settings**, then **Usage & Plans**, then **Manage Subscription**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Stopping Anyx

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I stop notes for a group?">
    Turn off **Generate notes for this group** in the group's **Notes** tab. Your existing notes stay available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I stop reminders?">
    Reply `STOP` (or `הפסק`) in your private chat with Anyx. Reply `START` (or `המשך`) to turn them back on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I delete my data?">
    Delete single memories in **Settings**, under **AI Brain**. To delete everything, email [support@anyx.app](mailto:support@anyx.app) from the address on your account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Didn't find your answer? See [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting) or email [support@anyx.app](mailto:support@anyx.app).
