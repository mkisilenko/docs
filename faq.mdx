## About Anyx

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Anyx?">
    Anyx is an AI agent that works inside WhatsApp. Add it to your WhatsApp groups and it emails you a short briefing of each group: decisions, unanswered questions, action items and what needs you. In a private chat, it is your personal assistant for reminders, routines and your connected apps. See [What is Anyx?](/)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Anyx made by WhatsApp?">
    No. Anyx, Inc. is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta. Anyx is a separate WhatsApp contact that you chat with and add to groups.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it work on iPhone, Android and WhatsApp Web?">
    Yes. Anyx is a regular WhatsApp contact, so it works in any WhatsApp app: iPhone, Android, WhatsApp Web and WhatsApp Desktop. There is nothing to install.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it work in Hebrew?">
    Yes. Anyx understands Hebrew, English and mixed chats. Notes are written in the language of the chat by default, or in one of 20 languages you choose.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can Anyx read all my WhatsApp chats?">
    No. Anyx is not connected to your WhatsApp account. It only sees your private chat with it and the groups you add it to. See [Privacy and security](/security).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do group members see my notes?">
    No. Notes go to your email and your Anyx account, and to any email recipients you add. They are never posted in the group.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Anyx use my data to train AI models?">
    Anyx does not train AI models on your data. To answer you and write notes, Anyx sends message content to third-party AI providers. For the legal details, see the [Privacy Policy](https://anyx.app/privacy).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should I tell the group that I added Anyx?">
    Yes. Only connect groups you administer, and let the members know Anyx is there. There is a ready-made message in [Connect a WhatsApp group](/ai-notes/connect-a-group).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## AI Notes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will Anyx talk in my group?">
    Only when someone tags it, for example `@Anyx`. Otherwise it stays quiet. On Pro and Max you can also set up a routine that runs for a group.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How often do notes arrive?">
    Every 24 hours by default. You can choose every 6, 12 or 48 hours, or once a week. The first note is processed on an upcoming scheduled run after you connect the group. If there were no messages during an interval, no email is sent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get notes on WhatsApp instead of email?">
    Automatic notes go by email. Every note in your account has a **Send to my WhatsApp** button that sends it to your private chat with Anyx.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Anyx summarize messages from before it joined?">
    Yes, if you import a WhatsApp chat export. Then ask for a note for that date range. See [Import older messages](/ai-notes/settings#import-older-messages).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it understand voice notes?">
    Yes. Voice notes are transcribed, so their content can appear in notes and in answers.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Plans and billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much does Anyx cost?">
    Starter is $35 a month (up to 4 groups), Pro is $60 (up to 30 groups) and Max is $120 (up to 120 groups). Yearly billing is 20% cheaper. See [Plans and free trial](/plans) and the [pricing page](https://anyx.app/pricing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Yes. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial on any plan. If you cancel before it ends, you pay nothing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free plan?">
    Groups, AI Notes and connected apps need Starter, Pro or Max. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I cancel?">
    On [anyx.app](https://anyx.app), open **Settings**, then **Usage & Plans**, then **Manage Subscription**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Stopping Anyx

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I stop notes for a group?">
    Turn off **Generate notes for this group** in the group's **Notes** tab. Your existing notes stay available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I stop reminders?">
    Reply `STOP` (or `הפסק`) in your private chat with Anyx. Reply `START` (or `המשך`) to turn them back on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I delete my data?">
    Delete single memories in **Settings**, under **AI Brain**. To delete everything, email [support@anyx.app](mailto:support@anyx.app) from the address on your account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
