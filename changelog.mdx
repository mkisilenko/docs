<Update label="September 2026" description="AI Notes for WhatsApp groups">
  **AI Notes is available on Starter, Pro and Max.** Add Anyx to a WhatsApp group and get an automatic briefing by email: decisions, unanswered questions, action items, opportunities and what needs you. Notes are on by default for newly connected groups.

  - Choose how often notes arrive: every 6, 12, 24 or 48 hours, or weekly.
  - Notes in the language of the chat, or in one of 20 languages.
  - Up to 5 email recipients per group.
  - **Worth checking**: optional questions about assumptions and risks.
  - Create a note for any past date range.
  - Import older messages from a WhatsApp chat export.
  - Copy notes as Markdown, or download them as `.md`, CSV or PDF.

  **Plans.** Starter connects up to 4 groups, Pro up to 30 and Max up to 120.

  **Docs.** These docs were rewritten to match the product as it works today.
</Update>
