<Update label="November 10, 2026" description="Read captured group messages with MCP and the API">
  **Message-level access for your AI and backend.** A compatible MCP client can list eligible active groups and read captured messages. Backend integrations can use the same personal key with the read-only API. Long-message responses preserve full stored text after link redaction, within the account's plan window.

  The key covers all eligible active groups owned by the account. Group choices in a connector are sync filters, not credential restrictions. Imported history, direct messages, Notes and attachment files are excluded; capture may be incomplete. Neither connection sends WhatsApp messages or writes to a CRM.

  See the [MCP quickstart](/developers/mcp-quickstart), [API reference](/developers/api-reference) and [OpenClaw/CRM guide](/developers/openclaw-crm).
</Update>

<Update label="November 2026" description="Use your notes with your AI assistant">
  **Guides for nine AI assistants.** Anyx already emails your group notes. New guides show how to use that email in ChatGPT and Codex, Claude and Claude Code, Gemini, Grok and Grok Bot, Instinct, Meta Muse, OpenClaw, NanoClaw and Hermes Agent: connect the mailbox, test one brief and, where your assistant supports it, schedule it. These are email handoffs, not live access to your WhatsApp groups. See [Use your Notes with an AI assistant](/ai-notes/assistants/overview).
</Update>

<Update label="October 2026" description="Tell Anyx what matters in a group">
  **What matters in this group?** In each group's Notes tab, set the group's context (personal or business), how important it is to you and your own priorities. It applies to future notes for that group. See [Schedule, language and recipients](/ai-notes/settings#tell-anyx-what-matters-in-this-group).
</Update>

<Update label="September 2026" description="AI Notes for WhatsApp groups">
  **AI Notes is available on Starter, Pro and Max.** Add Anyx to a WhatsApp group and get an automatic briefing by email: decisions, unanswered questions, action items, opportunities and what needs you. Notes are on by default for newly connected groups.

  - Choose how often notes arrive: every 6, 12, 24 or 48 hours, or weekly.
  - Notes in the language of the chat, or in one of 20 languages.
  - Up to 5 email recipients per group.
  - **Worth checking**: optional questions about assumptions and risks.
  - Create a note for any past date range.
  - Import older messages from a WhatsApp chat export.
  - Copy notes as Markdown, or download them as `.md`, CSV or PDF.
  - After you connect your number, Anyx asks whether to start with a group or with your personal assistant.

  **Plans.** Starter connects up to 4 groups, Pro up to 30 and Max up to 120.

  **Docs.** These docs were rewritten to match the product as it works today.
</Update>
