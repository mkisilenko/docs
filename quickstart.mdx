Set up Anyx and connect your first group in about 10 minutes. Your first briefing follows on an upcoming scheduled run, once the group has messages to summarize.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start your free trial">
    Go to the [pricing page](https://anyx.app/pricing), choose a plan and create your account with your email or Google. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial. If you cancel before it ends, you pay nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your WhatsApp number">
    Enter your WhatsApp number and the 6-digit code you receive by SMS, accept the Terms, and choose **Connect to Anyx**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say hi to Anyx on WhatsApp">
    Anyx is now waiting for you on WhatsApp. Reply to it, and save the number to your contacts. If you do not see a message after a few minutes, open [anyx.app](https://anyx.app) and use **Wake up Anyx**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your calendar and email (optional)">
    At the end of setup you can connect Google Calendar, Gmail or Outlook, so your assistant can check your schedule and inbox. You can also do this later. See [Connect your apps](/assistant/integrations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Anyx to your busiest group">
    Pick a work group you manage. Add Anyx as a member, then tag it once from your own number, for example `@Anyx hi`. Full steps: [Connect a WhatsApp group](/ai-notes/connect-a-group).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your first briefing">
    Your first note is processed on an upcoming scheduled run and emailed once there are messages to summarize. Delivery depends on activity and processing. To review a specific period, open the group's **Notes** tab on anyx.app and use **Generate notes for a date range**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Try your private assistant

While you wait for the first note, send Anyx a few messages in your private chat:

```text
Remind me tomorrow at 9:00 to call the accountant.
```

```text
What do I have on my calendar this week?
```

Or record a voice note with everything on your mind and ask for a to-do list.
