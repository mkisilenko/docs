Every Anyx plan includes AI Notes for your WhatsApp groups and a private assistant in WhatsApp. The main difference between plans is how many groups you can connect.

| Plan | Price | WhatsApp groups | App integrations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $35 / month | Up to 4 | Included |
| Pro | $60 / month | Up to 30 | Included |
| Max | $120 / month | Up to 120 | Unlimited |

Yearly billing is 20% cheaper than paying monthly. The [pricing page](https://anyx.app/pricing) always shows the current prices and is the source of truth if this page and the pricing page ever differ.

## Included in every plan

- **AI Notes** for each connected group: automatic briefings of decisions, open questions and action items, sent to your email.
- **Private assistant** in WhatsApp: reminders, routines, and tasks with your connected apps.
- **Voice notes, text and files** are included on every plan.

## The 7-day free trial

New subscribers get a 7-day free trial on any plan.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Pick a plan on the [pricing page](https://anyx.app/pricing) and start the trial.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a payment card">
    Checkout asks for a card. You are not charged during the 7 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel any time during the trial">
    If you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged. If you keep Anyx, the plan you chose starts automatically when the trial ends.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens when you reach your group limit

When you have connected as many groups as your plan allows, Anyx will not start working in a new group until you remove one or upgrade. Groups that are already connected keep working.

## Changing or canceling your plan

On [anyx.app](https://anyx.app), open **Settings**, then **Usage & Plans**, then **Manage Subscription** to upgrade, downgrade or cancel. If you move to a plan with fewer groups, the extra groups are paused. Upgrade again to restore them.
