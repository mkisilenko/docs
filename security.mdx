This page explains in plain words how Anyx handles your data. The [Privacy Policy](https://anyx.app/privacy) is the legal version and takes precedence.

## Anyx cannot see your other chats

Anyx is a separate WhatsApp contact. It is not connected to your WhatsApp account, so it cannot read your private chats or any group it is not a member of. It only receives what is sent to it directly, and the messages of groups it has been added to.

## What Anyx stores, and why

Anyx is a memory-based assistant. To remember, remind and write notes, it stores:

- **Your private chat with Anyx**, including voice notes, photos and documents you send.
- **Chat exports you upload**, to create the notes you ask for.
- **Messages of WhatsApp groups Anyx is a member of**, including voice-note transcriptions and the media content it can read, to write notes and to answer when someone tags it.
- **The notes it creates**, saved in your account.
- **Facts it remembers about you**, which you can review and delete in **Settings**, under **AI Brain**.

## Who can see what

| Data | Who can see it |
| --- | --- |
| Your private chat with Anyx | Only you |
| Notes from a group | You, in your account, and the email recipients you choose for that group |
| Group members | They see the group as usual. Notes are never posted to the group. |

Only connect groups you administer, and let the members know Anyx is there. See [Connect a WhatsApp group](/ai-notes/connect-a-group) for a message you can send.

## AI models

To understand messages and write notes, Anyx sends message content to third-party AI model providers. Your data is used only to provide the service to you. It is never sold and never used to train AI models.

## Connected apps

When you connect an app such as Gmail or Google Calendar, you sign in on the app's own page. Access is managed by a dedicated integration provider, so Anyx does not store your app passwords. Anyx reads app data when you ask for something or when a routine you created needs it, and does not copy your whole account. You can disconnect at any time. See [Connect your apps](/assistant/integrations).

## Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. Anyx never sees your full card details.

## How your data is protected

Data is sent over encrypted connections and used only to provide the service to you.

## How long data is kept

- Account data is kept while your account exists.
- Message content is kept for a limited period, for context and service quality.
- Group and notes data is kept while the group stays connected.

## Your controls

- **Pause notes** for any group, or stop automatic email. See [Schedule, language and recipients](/ai-notes/settings).
- **Remove Anyx** from a group like any other member.
- **Pause reminders and routines** by replying `STOP` in your private chat.
- **Delete what Anyx remembers**, item by item, in **Settings**, under **AI Brain**.
- **Delete everything.** Email [support@anyx.app](mailto:support@anyx.app) from the address on your account and ask us to delete your data.
