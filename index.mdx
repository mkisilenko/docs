**Anyx is an AI agent that works inside WhatsApp.** Add it to your work groups and it sends you a short briefing of each one: what was decided, which questions got no answer, who committed to what, and what needs you. You do not have to read every message.

In a private chat, Anyx is your personal assistant. Send it tasks, reminders and questions by text or voice, and connect your calendar and email so it can help with them.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your WhatsApp number">
    Start a free trial on [anyx.app](https://anyx.app) and connect your number. Anyx messages you on WhatsApp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Anyx to a group">
    Add Anyx to a group you manage and tag it once. After that it stays quiet unless someone tags it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your briefing">
    Anyx emails you a note for each group on a regular schedule, every 24 hours by default, whenever there were messages to summarize.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who it is for

- **Small business owners** who follow customers, suppliers and staff across several WhatsApp groups.
- **Team leads** whose project, shift or sales groups move faster than anyone can read.
- **Anyone with groups that matter**, like a building committee or a family group, who cannot read everything every day.

## What Anyx does not do

- It does not read your other WhatsApp chats. It only sees chats it is part of.
- It only knows what was written in the group after it joined, unless you [import a chat export](/ai-notes/settings#import-older-messages). Things said by phone or in person are not in the note.
- It does not post notes in the group.
