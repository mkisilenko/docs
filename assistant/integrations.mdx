## Connect an app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Integration Hub">
    Sign in on [anyx.app](https://anyx.app) and open the [Integration Hub](https://anyx.app/integrations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the app and the access level">
    **Read only** is recommended. With read only, Anyx can look things up. With full access, it can also make changes, like creating a draft or a calendar event. Only one access level can be active at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and approve">
    A window from the app itself opens (for example, Google). Sign in and approve the access. Anyx never sees your password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also connect Google Calendar, Gmail or Outlook at the end of sign-up.

## What to ask

```text
What do I have tomorrow afternoon?
```

```text
Find the last email from the accountant and summarize it.
```

```text
Draft a reply to Yossi saying we can meet on Tuesday. Show me before you send.
```

Anyx is set up to show you a draft and ask before it sends an email or changes something in an app.

## Disconnect or switch access

To disconnect, open the app in the Integration Hub and choose **Disconnect**. To switch between read only and full access, disconnect first and connect again. For Google, you can also remove Anyx's access from your Google Account security settings.

## If an app stops working

Apps sometimes end a connection, for example after you change your password. Disconnect the app in the Integration Hub and connect it again.
