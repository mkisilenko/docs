Your private chat with Anyx is your personal assistant. Write or talk to it like you would to a capable colleague. Only you see this chat.

## What you can send

- **Text**, in Hebrew, English or a mix.
- **Voice notes.** Anyx transcribes them in the background, so a reply to a long voice note can take a little longer.
- **Photos and documents.** Anyx reads them in the background and can then answer questions about them.
- **Links.** Send a link and ask for a summary, or ask Anyx to search the web.

## Try these

```text
Remind me on Thursday at 16:00 to send the quote to Avi.
```

```text
What do I have on my calendar tomorrow?
```

```text
Here is a voice note with everything on my mind. Turn it into a short to-do list.
```

```text
Summarize this article in 5 bullet points: https://example.com/article
```

## Before it sends anything

When a task means sending an email or changing something in one of your apps, Anyx is set up to show you a draft and ask you to confirm first. Read the draft before you say yes.

## In groups

In a WhatsApp group, Anyx answers only when someone tags it, for example `@Anyx what did we decide about the delivery date?` The rest of the time it stays quiet. See [Connect a WhatsApp group](/ai-notes/connect-a-group).

## Useful commands

| Send this | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| `/new` | Starts a fresh conversation. Anyx keeps your memory, notes and reminders. Use it if Anyx seems confused. |
| `STOP` (or `הפסק`) | Pauses reminders and routines in your private chat. |
| `START` (or `המשך`) | Turns them back on. |

Send the word on its own, as the whole message. Other slash commands are not available.

## Usage limits

Each plan includes a daily and monthly usage allowance. You can see how much you have used in **Settings**, under **Usage & Plans**. If you reach the limit, Anyx tells you.
